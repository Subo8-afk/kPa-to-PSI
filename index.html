How to Convert kPa to PSI

Pressure is measured using several different units depending on the country, industry, and type of equipment. Two of the most commonly encountered units are kilopascals (kPa) and pounds per square inch (PSI). Knowing how to convert between them is useful when reading tyre-pressure specifications, checking air compressors, working with pneumatic equipment, or comparing technical measurements.

Understanding the kPa to PSI Conversion

The kilopascal is part of the metric system and is widely used in scientific, engineering, and automotive applications. PSI, meaning pounds per square inch, is particularly common in the United States and in many automotive specifications.

To convert a pressure value from kPa to PSI, multiply the kPa value by 0.145038. This conversion factor allows the two units to be compared using the same pressure measurement.

PSI = kPa × 0.145038

Examples of Converting kPa to PSI

For example, if a pressure reading is 250 kPa:

250 × 0.145038 = 36.2595 PSI

Therefore, 250 kPa is approximately 36.26 PSI.

Some commonly encountered conversions include:

  • 100 kPa ≈ 14.50 PSI
  • 200 kPa ≈ 29.01 PSI
  • 220 kPa ≈ 31.91 PSI
  • 240 kPa ≈ 34.81 PSI
  • 250 kPa ≈ 36.26 PSI
  • 300 kPa ≈ 43.51 PSI
  • 350 kPa ≈ 50.76 PSI
  • 400 kPa ≈ 58.02 PSI

Why kPa to PSI Conversion Is Useful

Converting pressure units can be particularly helpful when different equipment uses different measurement systems. A vehicle manual may specify tyre pressure in PSI, while a digital pressure gauge may display kPa. Compressors, pumps, hydraulic equipment, and technical documents can also use different units.

For accurate results, it is important to use the correct conversion factor and to distinguish between the pressure units being used. For everyday applications, rounding the final result to two decimal places is usually sufficient, while technical applications may require additional precision.
