kPa to PSI Conversion Guide

Convert kilopascals (kPa) to pounds per square inch (PSI) using a simple formula and practical examples.

Quick conversion

To convert kPa to PSI, multiply the pressure in kilopascals by approximately 0.1450377.

PSI = kPa × 0.1450377377

For example, 200 kPa is approximately 29.01 PSI.

What do kPa and PSI mean?

Kilopascal (kPa) and pounds per square inch (PSI) are units used to measure pressure. Kilopascals are part of the metric system, while PSI is commonly used on pressure gauges and in vehicle tyre specifications in some countries.

Common uses for pressure conversion

  • Checking vehicle tyre pressure against a manufacturer's recommendation.
  • Comparing air compressor and pressure-washer specifications.
  • Reading technical documents that use different pressure units.
  • Working through engineering, science, and maintenance calculations.
