Tyre Pressure: kPa to PSI Guide

Tyre pressure specifications may be shown in kPa, PSI, or bar. Knowing how to convert the units helps you compare readings correctly.

Example: 220 kPa to PSI

220 × 0.1450377377 = approximately 31.91 PSI.

That is commonly rounded to 31.9 PSI, or about 32 PSI for a quick reference.

Where to find the recommended tyre pressure

Use the vehicle manufacturer's recommended pressure, commonly shown on a label inside the driver's door area, in the owner's manual, or in official vehicle information. The correct value depends on the vehicle and its load.

Check tyre pressure when tyres are cold

For a routine check, follow the vehicle manufacturer's instructions and measure the tyres when they are cold. Driving warms tyres and can increase the pressure reading. Do not reduce a warm tyre to the cold-pressure specification just because the reading is higher.
