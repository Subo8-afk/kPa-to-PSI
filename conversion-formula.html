kPa to PSI Formula and Examples

The conversion between kilopascals and pounds per square inch uses a fixed relationship between the two pressure units.

kPa to PSI formula

PSI = kPa × 0.1450377377

To convert a kPa value to PSI, multiply the number of kilopascals by 0.1450377377. For many everyday calculations, the rounded factor 0.145038 is sufficient.

PSI to kPa formula

kPa = PSI × 6.894757293

To convert in the opposite direction, multiply PSI by approximately 6.894757293.

Why can results look slightly different?

Small differences usually come from rounding. A calculator may display two, four, or six decimal places, but the underlying conversion should use a sufficiently precise factor before rounding the final display.
