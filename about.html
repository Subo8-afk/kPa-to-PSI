About This kPa to PSI Guide

This website provides a straightforward reference for converting pressure measurements between kilopascals (kPa) and pounds per square inch (PSI).

What you'll find here

The guide includes a conversion chart, a practical tyre-pressure article, and an explanation of the conversion formula. These pages are intended to make common pressure conversions easier to understand.

Conversion method

The pages use the relationship 1 kPa ≈ 0.1450377377 PSI. Results shown in examples and tables are rounded for readability, so the last decimal may vary depending on the display precision.

Important note about tyre pressure

This website explains unit conversion and does not replace vehicle-specific advice. Always follow the pressure recommendations provided by your vehicle manufacturer or equipment documentation.
